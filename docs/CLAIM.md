**Title:** `[Recipient] JSP-000907 — Lean 4 formalization of Erdős 1091 (formalizer)`

**Body:**

This is a **formalizer** claim for JSP-000907 (Erdős 1091). It is not a claim on the prover side.

### What is being claimed

A complete Lean 4 formalization of Theorem 4.1 of Alexeev–Putterman–Sawhney–Sellke–Valiant,
*Short proofs in combinatorics, probability and number theory II*, arXiv:2604.06609, §4 — the
explicit counterexample that answers Erdős 1091 in the negative.

Repository: https://github.com/noahbenjamin1994/erdos-1091-lean (Lean toolchain and Mathlib commit pinned in the repo).

### Statement

The formalized statement is taken **verbatim** from
[`google-deepmind/formal-conjectures` PR #5870](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/pull/5870),
which is statement-only — the theorem there is a `sorry`. This work supplies that proof; the
signature is unchanged, and a character-level comparison confirms it:

```lean
theorem erdos_1091.variants.counterexample :
    ∃ C : ℕ, ∀ m : ℕ, 1 ≤ m →
      ∃ (n : ℕ) (G : SimpleGraph (Fin n)),
        m ≤ n ∧ n ≤ C * m ∧ G.CliqueFree 4 ∧ G.chromaticNumber = 4 ∧
          (∀ H : G.Subgraph, H ≠ ⊤ → H.coe.chromaticNumber ≤ 3) ∧
          (∀ c : G.Cycle, c.chords.encard ≤ 10)
```

Because the statement was written by a third party rather than by us, the usual
"does the Lean statement say what the problem says" question does not rest on our own judgement.

### How to verify

```bash
lake exe cache get
LAKE_NUM_JOBS=2 lake build Erdos1091
grep -rnE '\bsorry\b|\badmit\b|native_decide' Erdos1091 FormalConjecturesForMathlib   # no output
```

- 217 declarations, **0 `sorry`**, no custom `axiom`, no `native_decide`.
- `Erdos1091/Audit.lean` prints 41 `#print axioms` lines during the build; every one reports only
  `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` (one reports fewer). A copy is checked in as
  `docs/axioms.txt` for comparison.
- Per-declaration status is generated by scanning the sources: `docs/STATUS.md`.

### Relationship to existing work

- The mathematics is entirely due to Alexeev, Putterman, Sawhney, Sellke and Valiant.
  **Prover credit belongs to them; this claim is for formalizer credit only.**
- The target statement and the `SimpleGraph.Cycle` / `Cycle.chords` API it is phrased in come from
  PR #5870 (author: stantheman0128). Those files are vendored in the repository under
  `FormalConjecturesForMathlib/` with their original copyright headers, and are **not** part of
  what is claimed here.
- Three sibling problems from the same paper (JSP-000820, JSP-000823, JSP-000946) were formalized
  and claimed by the paper's first author. This one was not.

### Scope

Only §4 of the paper is formalized. Three intermediate lemmas are stated slightly weaker than the
paper's (documented in the repository README) — the final theorem is unaffected and no hypothesis
was added anywhere.
